## The terminal-state gap — found by that correction

Chasing the discrepancy turned up a structural problem, not a bookkeeping slip. **#98, #112 and #113 had no legal way to finish.** Their deliverable is a written finding; `verify_gate.sh` Invariant A demands a commit referencing the issue. That needs a `verify_gate.sh` change, a `CLAUDE.md` lifecycle change and `gate_sweep.sh` coverage — code and governance work deserving its own plan and gate, not something to do quietly overnight. **Filed as #123.** Until it lands the three milestones rest on Team Lead judgement alone, which is a recorded and time-boxed gap rather than a silent one.
